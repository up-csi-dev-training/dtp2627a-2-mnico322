Song Description:
Only by Chasing the Wind is the thirty-first original 
soundtrack album to be released for Honkai: Star Rail.

It features a total of three soundtracks produced by the HOYO-MiX team, 
with Chevy on vocals as Robin Summeretto's singing voice.
